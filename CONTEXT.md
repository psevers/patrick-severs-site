# Context — patricksevers.com

Vocabulary for this repo. The domain is Patrick's public positioning; the terms below are the
site's own structure and claims, decided in the 2026-07-17 grilling session
(`docs/decisions.md` — that file rules on anything this one summarises).

## Positioning

- **The builder/GM claim** (redesign 2026-09-08, `docs/redesign-2026-09.md`) — the hero leads with
  it: "I build the AI training-data business inside your company, walk it into the frontier labs,
  and build the team that delivers." Primary bet is Head of GTM / VP Revenue; the wedge is Human
  Data. Copy that reads as sales-only or as builder-only is wrong.
- **The read** — the site's point of view: presence and proof win the deal; agents do the
  operating work so a small team can afford to be present.
- **Receipt** — a proof attached to a claim (a number, a named event, a repo). Every rule and
  every stat carries one.
- **The three receipts** — the home page's proof section: **The G2i record** (done),
  **The Human Data Operating Layer** (designed; Patrick's genericized final-round blueprint), and
  **The Agent Company** (doing; live, dated case study). Each has its own page.
- **How I operate** (`how-i-operate.html`) — six operating rules with receipts, the agent fleet,
  and the things built under Patrick's direction. Absorbed the former Agentic Systems and Products
  pages, which now redirect there.
- **Career timeline** (`career.html`) — the 25-year arc. Not a proof section.

## Site and surface

- Static HTML/CSS/JS under `src/`, no build step. `src/index.html` is the home page.
- **Design language** — "working whiteboard": Fraunces display, Source Sans 3 body, Caveat for
  whiteboard annotations only, IBM Plex Mono for receipts. Blue marker accent, paper ground with a
  faint grid, sticky-note callouts. Light mode primary; dark mode ("dark whiteboard") toggle
  persisted in `localStorage`.
- **Board** — a whiteboard diagram card (`.board`, with a Caveat `.board-tab`). SVG vocabulary is
  the `wb-*` classes in `style.css`. Every page carries at least one.
- **Resume PDF** — one-page, ATS-friendly, generated by `scripts/build_resume.py` into
  `src/assets/pdf/`. It's a build output; edit the script, not the PDF.
- **Contact surface** — email, LinkedIn, and the Google Calendar "book time" link. Headshot lives
  in the contact/footer section only, never the hero.
- Deploy and analytics: `docs/deploy.md` (Cloudflare Pages + Cloudflare Web Analytics).

## Confidentiality vocabulary

- **Published claim** — a figure or client name Patrick has already published under his own name
  on LinkedIn. Fair game: the "$10-15M → targeting $80M" framing; Microsoft/Meta/Coinbase/Discover
  as enterprise clients; the 122% / 2.7× / 21.7× ratios.
- **Source material** — `patrick-gtm-record/`, `patrick-builder-record/`, `patrick-agent-profile/`.
  Read for context; never a base to extend, and never copy exact dollar figures, named prospects,
  or vendor names out of them. G2i may be named; the confidentiality rail in `AGENTS.md` governs.

## Verification

No test harness by design. `/implement` verifies in-browser rather than driving `/tdd`.

## Avoid

- "portfolio" for the site as a whole (it replaces a resume — it's job-search-facing, and the
  separate GTM one-pager is `severs-gtm-site`); "pillar" or "deep-dive page" (pre-redesign
  vocabulary; say **receipt** and name the page); naming the company behind the operating-layer
  blueprint anywhere on the site; anonymised phrasing for the softball-recruiting-tool story — it
  stays specific, Patrick's call.
