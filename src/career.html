Career overview · 1999 to now

The career behind the work.

From enterprise sales to leading revenue teams and new business lines. The roles, the transitions, and what each chapter taught me.

Seven eras

1999 to 2012
Dell
Senior Account Manager → Financial Services Sales Manager → Inside Sales Manager

Twelve years learning the fundamentals, then a ceiling.

Individual quota ownership through people management: account management, financial-services sales leadership, and inside-sales management at enterprise scale. By 2012, going deeper meant staying inside one product line for good. I wanted to test those skills in a different kind of business.

2012 to 2016
ServiceSource
Senior Sales Manager → Director → Senior Director, Account Management

Chose speed and pressure on purpose.

A faster, more competitive outsourcing environment built around constant execution against daily, monthly, and quarterly targets. Ran renewals and expansion for clients including Google and Motorola, and launched programs across Singapore, Europe, and the UK. Four years of travel and scale that stretched my range as a leader.

2016 to 2018
ADP
Workforce Management Consultant

Traded 300,000 air miles for a President's Club win closer to home.

After a year of nonstop global travel, a reset: local territory, enterprise HR and payroll consulting, and time to be home. Won President's Club along the way. The slower pace didn't cost performance.

2018 to 2020
Box
Enterprise Account Executive

Back to individual contributor, on purpose.

Traded management for an enterprise SaaS quota at a public company, selling content management and integration ecosystems to CIOs and CTOs. Closed the largest deal of that run with a full enterprise stack plus an integration partner ecosystem. First fluency in the technical weeds: APIs, data flow, unstructured content.

2020 to 2023
Braintrust
Director of Enterprise Account Expansion → VP, Client Development

The head-of-revenue shot that hadn't come yet.

Joined a talent-network startup rebuilding how companies staffed engineering talent after COVID, on intentionally thin margins. The draw was running a revenue organization instead of reporting into one. Helped take the business from cash-burning to scale, before a pivot to a SaaS product changed what the role was.

2023 to 2026
G2i
Director of Sales & Partnerships → Senior Director → Head of Revenue & Human Data

From sales leadership to running the business line.

Built G2i's frontier-lab relationships and took the business direct into human data, running the line cross-functionally with finance, talent, and engineering. Spent the last year going deep on agentic systems: how agents work inside a business, and how to talk about that with both business leaders and research teams. Left in July 2026.

2026, now
The Agent Company
GTM lead, consulting engagement

Doing the job in public while I look for the next one.

Running go-to-market for a startup putting AI agents into small-business operations. No CRM, no lead source, and no chosen product on day one. Five demos and four paid pilots by day 45. The working notes are live and dated. Looking for the role that needs the whole arc at once: the revenue leader, the general manager, and the AI operator.
