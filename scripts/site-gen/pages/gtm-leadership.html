Presence was the top of the funnel.

Cold outbound to frontier-lab researchers doesn't work. Being where they are does.

Conferences the team produced. React Miami and AI Engineer Miami, end to end. Presence at AI Engineer San Francisco and London Tech Week.
Dinners. VIP dinners in London, New York, and Paris. The part of the funnel no dashboard captures.
Creator channel. Feeding recruiting and human-data pipelines at once.

proof it workedQ2 2026: 1,075 leads, 59% of the quarter, led by AI Engineer World's Fair.

02

The human-data layer.

The company already owned the hard assets and an expert network of engineering. The gap was organizing cohorts into what the labs wanted.

Define the expertise. What are the labs looking for? Engineering expertise, reinforcement learning environments, senior-level human data experts.
Add only the AI-specific owners. GTM, program ops, expert ops — a small team with clear seats, not a giant org on day one.
Sell trusted outputs, not access. The market pays for results that improve models, not for a rolodex of experts.
03

Operating discipline that didn't exist before.

The staffing business ran on instinct and spreadsheets. It left running on a system.

Live RevOps. A real-time pipeline dashboard, an automated weekly leadership brief, and a forecast whose thresholds are tunable without a code change.
Trusted data underneath it. Lead database and hygiene rebuilt so the reporting could actually be forecast on.
Paid acquisition on ROI, not gut. Proved the channel, scaled while the return held, walked away when it didn't.
