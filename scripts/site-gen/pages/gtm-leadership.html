01

Built new revenue lines from zero.

G2i entered this window as one motion: professional staffing. It left as four, and the human-data line is the one the company's trajectory now rides on.

one to four
Human data with frontier labs. Moved the business from pure staffing to going direct with an in-house human-data and RL-environment stack for frontier model labs. We deployed 100 engineers in under two days for one lab's code-testing project.
Enterprise and frontier-lab accounts. The leading frontier labs, and enterprises including Microsoft, Meta, Coinbase, and Discover.
Managed services. Launched as a first-class line: full engagements for enterprise and lab customers, not placed engineers.
Orchestrator. A patent-pending, SOC2-compliant multi-agent orchestration product for complex engineering work, debuted publicly at the AI Engineer World's Fair.
02

Ran it cross-functionally. That's the GM part.

The growth numbers are the output. The work was leading the effort across finance, talent, engineering, and go-to-market, and hiring and placing the right people in each function to execute it.

With finance: the model, the targets, and the unit economics that kept growth profitable. Margin held while the mix shifted from staffing to human data. This wasn't growth bought with margin.
With talent: hiring the staff-level engineers who do the lab work, and building the pipeline of them ahead of demand.
With engineering: technical framing, lab overviews, delivery. Being able to sit in the room with a research team and be useful.
With go-to-market: the sales team, the executive relationships, and the events engine that fed the top of the funnel.
what the role touched
03

The events engine was the top of the funnel.

Cold outbound to frontier-lab researchers doesn't work. Being where they are does. So the team ran conferences, hosted dinners, and built a creator channel, and it became the largest lead source in the business.

Conferences the team produced. React Miami and AI Engineer Miami, end to end. Presence at AI Engineer San Francisco and London Tech Week.
Dinners and relationships. VIP dinners in London, New York, and Paris. Executive conversations and being on the ground: the part of the funnel no dashboard captures, and where much of the pipeline started.
The creator channel. An influencer and creator program feeding both the recruiting and the human-data pipelines at once.
proof it workedEvents were the single largest lead source in Q2 2026: 1,075 leads, 59% of the quarter, led by AI Engineer World's Fair.
04

Built the operating discipline that didn't exist before.

The staffing business ran on instinct and spreadsheets. It left running on a system, most of it designed under my direction and much of it built by agents I run.

First live RevOps reporting. A real-time pipeline dashboard, an automated weekly leadership brief, and a forecast model whose stage probabilities and health thresholds are tunable without a code change.
Fixed the data underneath it. Rebuilt the lead database and data hygiene so the reporting could actually be trusted and forecast on.
Paid acquisition on an ROI model, not gut feel. Proved the channel, scaled it only while the return held, and walked away when the economics stopped working.
Built-vs-buy on RevOps. Brought in an outsourced RevOps team running the systems at a fraction of the in-house cost.
Extended the CRM's data model to carry two brand-new business lines as first-class objects.
05

The read, and where it's headed.

We stopped defending a flat staffing market and concentrated on the opportunity with the highest ceiling, frontier-lab human data. That focus produced the growth. Revenue scaled while margin held, so profit grew alongside the top line. One quarter of 2026 did 2.7 times the revenue of all of 2024.

On the trajectory set in this window, G2i is targeting roughly $80M in 2026 revenue, up from a $10-15M base three years earlier, carried by the human-data ramp with the leading frontier labs and enterprises including Microsoft, Meta, Coinbase, and Discover.
